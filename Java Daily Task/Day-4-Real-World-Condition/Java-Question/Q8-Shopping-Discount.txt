Q08 - SHOPPING DISCOUNT
-----------------------
Get the shopping amount and customer membership type.

Membership types:
1. Premium
2. Regular
3. Non-Member

Discount:
Premium:
- Amount >= Rs.5000 -> 25%
- Otherwise -> 20%

Regular:
- Amount >= Rs.5000 -> 15%
- Otherwise -> 10%

Non-Member:
- Amount >= Rs.5000 -> 5%
- Otherwise -> No Discount

Display discount and final amount.
